---
title: "Thematic Motifs for Characters"
date: 2026-01-04
excerpt: "Using short motifs to create memorable character identities in games."
layout: default
---

Short motifs are powerful tools for giving characters a musical identity. A two- or four-bar idea can be transformed across situations — varied in tempo, harmony, or orchestration — to reflect a character's arc.

Write motifs that are flexible: ensure they work in different keys and can be reharmonized. In interactive scenes, trigger motif variations to underline player choices or emotional beats, tying music closely to narrative.

Keep motifs small and distinct so they are recognizable when they return; small melodic contours often register more strongly than complex themes in gameplay contexts.
