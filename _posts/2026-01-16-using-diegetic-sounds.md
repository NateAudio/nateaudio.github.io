---
title: "Using Diegetic Sounds Musically"
date: 2026-01-16
excerpt: "Integrating in-world sounds as musical elements to deepen immersion."
layout: default
---

Diegetic sounds — music that originates in the game world — can blur the line between score and environment. A tavern minstrel, an in-game radio, or rhythmic machinery can all serve as musical sources.

Treat diegetic elements as composers do: arrange them, EQ them for clarity, and use their motifs to connect to the non-diegetic score. Crossfading between diegetic and non-diegetic versions of the same theme strengthens narrative cohesion.

Be mindful of player control: if the player can change or move diegetic sources, ensure the music system adapts cleanly so immersion is preserved.
