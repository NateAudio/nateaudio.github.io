---
title: "Mixing for Games vs Mixing for Albums"
date: 2026-01-19
excerpt: "Key differences when preparing music for interactive playback versus a linear album."
layout: default
---

Game mixes must coexist with SFX and dialogue; that changes balance, frequency choices, and dynamic range decisions. While album mixes can be tailored for a single listening context, game mixes should prioritize clarity and flexibility.

Use sidechain or ducking techniques to make room for important audio events, and keep lead frequencies clear for motifs that need to be recognizable during gameplay. Consider multiple stems and render-ready assets to let engineers adapt levels in-engine.

Finally, test mixes in the engine with real SFX and dialogue — what sounds full in a DAW may be buried once in-game elements are present.
