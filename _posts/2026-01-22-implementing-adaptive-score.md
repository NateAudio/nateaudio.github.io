---
title: "Implementing an Adaptive Score"
date: 2026-01-22
excerpt: "Practical steps for turning composed music into an adaptive in-game score."
layout: default
---

Implementing an adaptive score starts with organizing your music into clear, usable assets: stems, cues, and short transition pieces. Keep stems musically compatible so they can be layered without dissonance.

Work closely with designers to map emotional states and gameplay flags to musical transitions. Use middleware to manage cues and test transitions under gameplay to avoid abrupt musical jumps.

Document your cues, naming conventions, and expected behaviour so engineers can integrate the music reliably and QA can reproduce transitions for polish.
