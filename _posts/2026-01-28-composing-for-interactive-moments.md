---
title: "Composing for Interactive Moments"
date: 2026-01-28
excerpt: "Strategies for scoring short interactive beats and player-driven moments."
layout: default
---

Interactive moments — a discovery, a boss reveal, a player-triggered event — benefit from short, high-impact musical cues. These cues should be concise, clear in purpose, and designed to layer on top of the existing score.

Design cues with immediate identity: a short motif, a distinctive chord hit, or a rhythmic groove that can be introduced and then blended back into the background. Keep duration flexible so the engine can extend or shorten the cue as needed.

Work with engineers to define entry and exit points and provide short transition loops or fills to smooth state changes. Good interactive cues enhance feedback without distracting from gameplay.
